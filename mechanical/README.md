# Printed front panel + box (Bambu Lab P1S)

Generated by `mechanical/panel_box.py` from `hardware/panel/layout.json` (the approved v4
layout). Regenerate after changing a parameter at the top of the script:

```
"C:\Program Files\FreeCAD 1.0\bin\freecadcmd.exe" mechanical/panel_box.py
python mechanical/tests/test_mechanical.py          # holes = layout, closed meshes, fits P1S
uv run --with numpy --with matplotlib mechanical/render_previews.py
```

## Files (`mechanical/out/`, already in print orientation)

| File | Print | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `panel.stl` | face **down**, no supports | smooth front from the plate. 160 × 90 × 3 mm + 4 mm of features on the back |
| `panel_labels.stl` | with the panel | same origin: Bambu Studio → right-click panel → *Add part* → this file, give it white filament (AMS). No AMS: leave it out, the labels stay engraved |
| `box.stl` | standing on its **back wall**, no supports | 166.4 × 96.4 × 130 mm, 3 mm walls |
| `oled_clamp.stl` | flat | holds the OLED module in its pocket |
| `*.step` | — | for editing in FreeCAD / Fusion |

Suggested: PETG or PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3–4 walls, 15 % infill; textured PEI plate gives
the panel front a nice finish.

## Parts that mount in the panel (sizes = script parameters)

| Part | Hole | Parameter |
|---|---|---|
| 4 × KY-040 rotary encoder (EC11, M7 bushing) | Ø7.5 | `ENCODER_HOLE` |
| 5 × 7 mm panel-mount tactile pushbutton (Scan, Manual, DR P4, Randers, Back) | Ø7.2 | `BUTTON_HOLE` |
| 7 × 3 mm LED, pressed in from the back (collar Ø5 × 4 mm) | Ø3.1 | `LED_HOLE`, `LED_COLLAR` |
| 1.5" 128×128 SH1107 OLED module | 30 × 30 window, pocket for the module board | `OLED_WINDOW`, `OLED_MODULE` |
| 3.5 mm panel jack (PJ-392, M6) for headphones | Ø6.2 | `JACK_HOLE` |

**Measure before printing:** the OLED module board (`OLED_MODULE` = 36 × 36 mm, 4 mm thick
assumed) and which side its 4 pins are on (`OLED_HEADER_SIDE`), and the hole size of your
pushbuttons. Everything else is standard.

## Box

* Panel drops flush into the front opening; 6 × **M2.5 × 8** countersunk screws into the ribs.
* Raspberry Pi 3B on 4 printed standoffs (**M2.5 × 6** self-tapping), USB/Ethernet end
  against the back-wall window: plug the RTL-SDR (on its USB extension) and the USB speaker
  straight into the Pi.
* Back wall: **USB-C** cutout 12.6 × 6.6 mm with 2 × Ø3.2 holes 22 mm apart for a
  panel-mount USB-C → micro-USB cable (check your part's hole spacing: `ux`, ±11 mm in
  `build_box`), and an **Ø8.1** hole for a 5.5 × 2.1 mm DC jack = LNA 5 V output.
* Vents over the Pi (top and side).
* Space behind the panel ~37 mm before the Pi's front edge for the KY-040 modules and wiring.

## Wiring

The panel is hand-wired to the owner's own HAT (one pin header per control). KY-040 modules
are 3.3 V: their `+` pin goes to the Pi's **3.3 V**, never 5 V.
